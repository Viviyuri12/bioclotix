The Critical Challenge

Every Second Dictates Survival.

Uncontrolled hemorrhage remains the leading cause of preventable death in trauma scenarios, accounting for roughly 35% to 40% of all pre-hospital trauma fatalities. Traditional haemostatic products frequently fall short in chaotic field environments due to high manufacturing costs, complex application requirements, and an inability to effectively conform to deep, irregular wound pockets.

Furthermore, existing single-powder or liquid delivery systems suffer from premature moisture clumping and poor stability, creating an urgent medical need for an accessible, affordable, and precisely deployable solution.

Our Proposed Solution

Meet BIOCLOTIX: Dual-Biomaterial Precision.

BIOCLOTIX is an innovative, plant-derived dry powder haemostatic system engineered to bridge the gap between high-tech hospital care and rugged, accessible field medicine.

By storing Oxidised Regenerated Cellulose (ORC) and Microporous Starch in completely separate chambers until the exact moment of use, the applicator eliminates premature moisture clumping and propels both powders simultaneously through an adjustable airflow nozzle.

Four Core Design Objectives

1. Clinical Safety 2. Ultra-Affordability 3. 100% Biodegradability 4. Rapid Haemostasis
System Architecture

Components of BIOCLOTIX

Two complementary plant-derived biomaterials paired with a purely mechanical dual-chamber delivery system.

01

ORC Powder

Plant-derived Oxidised Regenerated Cellulose fiber that forms a supportive structural matrix and accelerates natural blood clotting.

02

Microporous Starch

An ultra-absorbent plant polysaccharide whose microscopic pores rapidly wick away fluid to concentrate red blood cells and platelets.

03

Dual-Chamber Body

Isolated internal compartments keep the two powders completely separate prior to deployment to prevent moisture exposure and clumping.

04

Nozzle & Manual Pump

Non-electronic airflow mechanism with rotating Small, Medium, Large, and Off settings for targeted wound coverage.

Coordinated Mechanism

How It Works

When the manual pump is compressed, controlled airflow propels the ORC and starch powders through separate internal channels. They deploy simultaneously through the nozzle, where the starch instantly absorbs excess fluid to concentrate blood components, while the ORC provides an immediate matrix to support the body's natural clot formation.

Real-World Viability

Practicality & Cost Economics.

BIOCLOTIX is engineered for rugged, real-world deployment by prioritizing low cost and mechanical simplicity. Using plant-derived ORC and microporous starch keeps the raw material cost at roughly 1.00 to 1.30 AED per application, enabling affordable mass stockpiling.

The dry powder system requires zero electricity or refrigeration, staying shelf-stable at room temperature for years. Its purely mechanical pump requires no medical training—any bystander or responder can point and spray instantly—while the biodegradable materials break down cleanly without the painful scab-tearing of standard gauze.

Project Conclusion

Nature-Inspired Chemistry Meets Functional Engineering.

BIOCLOTIX bridges the gap between advanced biomaterial science and practical, everyday engineering. By pairing plant-derived oxidized regenerated cellulose and microporous starch within a purpose-built dual-chamber applicator, it offers a concept that is affordable, shelf-stable, biodegradable, and intuitive enough for use anywhere from surgical theaters to roadside emergencies. While clinical validation and regulatory testing remain essential next steps, BIOCLOTIX demonstrates how thoughtful mechanical design and nature-inspired chemistry can work together to make rapid bleeding control accessible when every second counts.
