Two synergistic plant-derived powders housed inside an isolated mechanical delivery system for immediate, moisture-protected deployment.

Operational Durability

Practicality & Field Readiness

BIOCLOTIX is purpose-built for demanding real-world environments where conventional medical supplies fail. Because the dry powder formula requires zero electricity or refrigeration, it maintains long-term ambient shelf stability in ambulances, remote clinics, and field trauma packs, while relying on abundant plant-derived ingredients keeps the system practical and affordable for widespread deployment.

Its compact, lightweight bottle fits seamlessly into standard first-aid kits and operates via intuitive single-handed pumping—allowing responders or bystanders to stop bleeding immediately without specialized training or painful dressing removal later.

Zero Power
Fully Mechanical Operation

Operates entirely on manual airflow compression with no batteries, wires, or external power sources required.

Ambient Stable
No Cold-Chain Required

Moisture-isolated dry powder chambers prevent premature clumping and eliminate refrigeration across extreme climates.

Point & Pump
Intuitive Single-Handed Use

Lightweight ergonomic housing requires no mixing or setup, enabling immediate action under high-stress conditions.

Biodegradable
Breaks Down Naturally Without Scab Tearing

Plant-derived cellulose and starch absorb safely and clear gently with saline—preventing secondary re-bleeding.
